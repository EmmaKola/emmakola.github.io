About Me

I am a data analyst who enjoys turning raw, complex, and messy datasets into clear stories that help people make better decisions.

My work focuses on building interactive dashboards, modelling data, automating reporting workflows, and translating analysis into recommendations that stakeholders can act on.

I use tools such as SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel to clean, transform, analyse, and communicate data. I am especially interested in business intelligence, performance reporting, data storytelling, and dashboard design.

My portfolio includes analytics projects across sales performance, pharmaceutical distribution, customer segmentation, operational efficiency, and business reporting.
